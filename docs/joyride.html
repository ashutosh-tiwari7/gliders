Why we say safest

Anyone can sell you a flight. We'd rather show you the checklist.

Manali has a lot of operators and not all of them fly by the book. Here is exactly what happens before your feet leave the ground — ask any operator in the valley the same questions.

  1. 01

    Pilots who are actually certified

    Every tandem pilot holds a current APPI or DGCA-recognised tandem rating, carries their licence on them, and has logged a minimum of 500 tandem flights before they ever fly a guest. Ask to see the card — we like being asked.

  2. 02

    Gear with a paper trail

    EN-certified tandem wings retired at 300 hours regardless of how they look. Reserve parachutes repacked every 90 days with the date written on the outside. Every harness, carabiner and helmet logged with a serial number and an inspection date.

  3. 03

    A real no-fly rule

    Wind window checked at launch, mid-station and landing before the first flight of the day, then every hour. If the numbers are wrong we ground everything and refund you in full. We cancel roughly one day in nine during season — that is the point.

  4. 04

    Radio, ground crew, insurance

    Your pilot is on radio with the landing zone for the entire flight. A first-aid-certified crew member is on the meadow whenever a wing is in the air. Every guest is covered by accident insurance included in the price, not sold as an extra.

Run before every flight

Eight items. No exceptions.

  • Pilot licence & tandem rating verified
  • Reserve parachute repack date under 90 days
  • Wing hours logged and within limits
  • Harness, carabiners & helmet serial-checked
  • Wind readings at launch, mid & landing
  • Guest weight matched to wing and pilot
  • Radio comms tested with ground crew
  • Insurance active on the day of flight
