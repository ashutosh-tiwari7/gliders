Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Gliders Cafe & Adventures handles your personal data when you use this website or book with us. It is written to comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Data we collect

  • Booking details: your name, phone number, chosen activity, date, time and number of guests.
  • Messages: anything you send us by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • On-site information: weight, age and health declarations, and the signed waiver form, collected at our counter for safety.
  • Usage data: device, browser, pages visited and approximate location, collected through cookies and analytics or advertising tools (for example Google Analytics and Google Ads).

2. Why we use it

  • To confirm, schedule and run your booking, and to contact you about it.
  • To keep you safe and meet our legal and insurance obligations.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To measure and show relevant ads, including remarketing on Google and Meta platforms. You can opt out at any time (see section 5).

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (hosting, booking systems, messaging, analytics and advertising), with insurers in case of an incident, or when the law requires it.

4. Retention and security

We keep booking data for as long as we need it to provide the service and meet legal, tax and insurance requirements. Then we delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

5. Cookies and advertising

We and our partners, including Google, use cookies to analyse traffic and personalise ads. You can control cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com. To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Write to fly@gliderscafe.com. We reply within 30 days.

7. Children

We collect data about guests under 18 only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

8. Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, Gliders Cafe & Adventures, Gliders Cafe & Adventures, NH-3 Manali Highway, Dobhi, Kullu, Himachal Pradesh 175131. Email: fly@gliderscafe.com. Phone: +91 87882 47427.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the current version.
